# Motion

Ship a small, intentional motion set:

1. **Player mount** — soft fade-in when preview becomes ready.
2. **Status change** — short crossfade on status chip updates (`rendering` → `needs_review`).
3. **Approve success** — brief confirmation (not confetti); then navigate/highlight Final slot on Content board.

Avoid continuous background parallax, glow pulses, or motion that competes with video preview.
