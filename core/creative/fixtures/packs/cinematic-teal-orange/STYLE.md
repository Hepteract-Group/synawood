# Cinematic teal-orange

A restrained cinematic grade for short-form ads and product cuts.

## Look

- Shadows lean teal; highlights lean warm orange.
- Prefer restrained saturation; avoid neon glow.
- Keep skin tones natural; push color in backgrounds and product surfaces first.

## Pairing

- Works well with Hooks first 3s for ≤60s ad cuts.
- Use when the Brief asks for premium or filmic mood without heavy VFX.
