# Runbook: {Name}

**Purpose:** What outcome this process produces and why it exists.
**Cadence:** How often it runs (e.g. weekly, Monday).
**Owner:** Role, not person (e.g. "marketing operator").
**Time budget:** Expected duration for one run.
**Automation status:** manual | partially automated | automated (link to job)

## Inputs

- What must exist before starting (files, access, data, prior outputs).

## Steps

1. Numbered, specific, executable by someone who has never done this before.
2. Each step states *what done looks like*.
3. Steps that are automated link to the automation and describe what it does.

## Outputs

- Artifacts produced (files, scheduled posts, reports) and where they live.

## Escalation

- What to do when a step fails or an input is missing.
- When to stop and ask the founder instead of improvising.

## Change log

- YYYY-MM-DD — what changed and why.
