# Cognitive economy

At every moment the viewer must know what to look at.

## Load

Do not stack narration + giant kinetic type + infographic + SFX + busy background. Cut until one hero remains.

## Hierarchy

- Primary: notice first
- Secondary: explains it
- Tertiary: can wait (or go)

Carousel: one statement per slide. Not 73 words and four stats on every card.

## Complementarity

Bad: VO, title, and graphic all say “SAVE THREE HOURS EVERY DAY.”  
Better: VO names the morning hunt; picture closes tabs; type shows `3 hours → 10 minutes`.

Silence is allowed when picture carries meaning.

## Mute

Captions + picture must carry the message. Karaoke captions are the default when there is speech, not decoration.

## Done when

You can point at the frame and name the one thing that matters.
