# Concept diversity

## Not a new concept

Blue headline → red headline. Different cast. Different bed music. Same scene plan.

## A new concept

Pick a different *mechanism*:

- Problem agitation
- Product demonstration
- Customer story
- Contrarian claim
- Comparison
- Founder explanation
- Before/after
- Educational insight
- Humour
- Social proof

When they ask for “more versions,” propose at least two of these before spending generate budget.

## Hypothesis

Each concept states what it is testing (“missed tenders vs time saved”). Recolor cannot carry a new hypothesis.

## Done when

Two cuts for the same product would not share the same scene list if you stripped colour and music.
