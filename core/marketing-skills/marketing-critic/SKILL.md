# Marketing critic

Eyes on frames. Not a second agent. Rank: communication → relevance → persuasion → clarity → brand → platform fit → aesthetics.

## Fail the cut when

- You cannot say who it is for, or it is for “everyone”
- You cannot say the one message after ~2 seconds of the open
- No benefit / outcome is visible or readable
- A strong claim has no Catalog / Extract proof in picture
- Hierarchy is gone (competing type + VO + chart)
- Muted, the message dies (no captions, no visual story)
- Audio only repeats the title card
- Objective needed a CTA and the last seconds have none
- It looks like a recut 16:9 TV spot in a 9:16 hole
- Brand is only a pulsing logo
- Extra information fights the one idea
- The open is logo + company intro + “in today’s rapidly changing world”

## Still fail (editor craft)

Black frames, unreadable type, missing product on the proof beat — see `editor-critic`.

## Pass notes

Name what would make a target viewer scroll away. If nothing, say that.
