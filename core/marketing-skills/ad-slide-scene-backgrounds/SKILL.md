# Scene backgrounds

Brand (non-negotiable): use this project's Brand Studio — logo, primary/accent, fonts, CTA, claims. Do not invent a palette. Do not fake product UI. Path C / set_slide carries headlines; generated pixels are scenes, not typography.

## Rules

Each slide is a new frame of a story, not a restyled title card.

- Vary location, time of day, and subject. Do not reuse one office desk six times.
- Prefer real light, hands, documents, streets — specific, not "abstract concept".
- Keep brand colors as overlays and bugs, not the entire canvas.
- Prompt generate_slide_background with the headline's idea, not the headline text painted in the image.

## Tools

Use Studio Tools only. No shell, no vendor CLIs, no skills.sh scripts.
