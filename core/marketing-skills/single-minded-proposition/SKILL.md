# Single-minded proposition

## Fields (say them, then shoot them)

- **PRIMARY_MESSAGE** — the one thing to remember (`intent.primaryMessage`)
- **SUPPORTING_POINTS** — maximum two (`intent.supportingPoints`)
- **CTA** — one action that matches the objective (`intent.cta`; do not invent a second CTA)

Write them with `set_intent` before or with `write_composition`. Feature-dump headlines (“Fast! Easy! AI-powered!”) get rewritten on Intent, not shipped.

## Benefit ladder

Feature → functional benefit → economic/social benefit → emotional consequence.

“AI monitors tender portals” is a feature.  
“You stop hunting 14 sites every morning” is the ad.

## Fail

Fast! Easy! Powerful! AI-powered! Secure! Affordable! Automated! Innovative!

Three unrelated promises across hook, body, and end card.

## Done when

A muted 3-second hold of the hook still communicates one idea.
