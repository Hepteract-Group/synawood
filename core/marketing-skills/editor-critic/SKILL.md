# Editor critic

These rules are for looking at player frames, not for chatting.

## Never black

- Fail coverage if any sampled frame is empty, black, or missing main picture.
- Music or voice over black is an audio fail.
- A tiny stamp in the corner on black is a size fail. Main picture should fill the frame unless they asked for an overlay.

## Product on screen

- During the proof beat, the product (or a still of the product) should be readable on screen.
- Generic stock with no product is a brand fail.
- Fail brief if the source product, garment, or prop is not recognisable — a colour-only match is a fail. Clothes, phones, websites, and other materials must still look like the photo in the media bin.

## Readable type

- Hook titles, captions, and end cards must be large enough to read on a phone.
- Type that sits on busy picture without contrast is a fail.
- Fail brief if a hook or end card appears in the **middle** of the ad. Hook is the first seconds. End card is the last seconds, after picture, not over the middle of the cut.

## One look

- Fail brief if outfits, models, or identity change across sampled frames unless they asked for a wardrobe change.
- Fail brief if the source product, garment, or prop is not recognisable — a colour-only match is a fail. Clothes, phones, websites, and other materials must still look like the photo in the media bin.

## Pacing

- A “make a video” request that is only stills is a motion fail, unless they asked for a slideshow.
- Dead air on the picture track is a coverage fail. Cuts should feel continuous.

## Brand look

- Colour, logo, and type should match this product’s kit / DNA, not a generic template.
- A pulsing logo loop is not brand linkage — product UI or distinctive kit should appear on the proof beat.
- Claims on screen must stay inside the catalog.
