# Vibe: informative

## Pacing
- Clear steps. Even beats per point. Don't rush explanations.

## Visual
- Text-first: readable overlays. Prefer solution/proof scenes to land features.

## Captions
- Higher density OK when listing benefits. Prefer short parallel phrases.

## Sound
- Neutral bed. Clarity over mood.

## Mutation bias
- Prefer captions and structure over aggressive trims. Pack for clarity, not hype.
