# Vibe: premium

## Pacing
- Prefer fewer, longer holds. Avoid jump cuts unless a hard problem/solution beat.
- Trim only dead air; keep breathing room after key claims.

## Visual
- Cool/neutral grade; brand primary as accent, not neon flood.
- End card clean: logo + one CTA line. No busy lower-thirds mid-cut.

## Captions
- Sparse. Short phrases. High contrast on solid or soft brand plate.

## Sound (when present)
- Low, steady bed. No hype risers. Duck under VO.

## Mutation bias
- Prefer `pack_clips` gently, light trims, align end card - not aggressive shorten-all.
