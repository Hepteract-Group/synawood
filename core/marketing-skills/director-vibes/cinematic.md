# Vibe: cinematic

## Pacing
- Intentional holds. Let emotional beats breathe. Soft opens, clear close.

## Visual
- Wider grading latitude. Prefer story order: hook → problem → solution → CTA.

## Captions
- Minimal during emotional peaks; readable on key lines only.

## Sound
- Atmospheric pad or sparse piano-like beds; no constant beat aggression.

## Mutation bias
- Avoid over-trimming. Prefer scene-order clarity and end-card landing.
