# Claim vs catalog

## Rules

- Only state benefits that appear in product marketing / ExtractedBrief — do not invent proof, rankings, testimonials, or compliance guarantees.
- Advertising law cares about the *impression* a viewer takes, not the marketer’s private intent. Soften or drop a line that would read as a measured claim without Catalog evidence.
- Forbidden patterns (reject or rewrite): "HIPAA compliant", "SOC 2 certified", "guaranteed", "#1", "never fails", medical cure claims, invented customer counts.
- Prefer concrete job-to-be-done language ("Edit a PDF without Adobe") over superlatives.
- If the founder pastes a risky claim, rewrite to a safer equivalent and say what changed.
- Product overlays (e.g. `privacy-claim-safety`) add stricter rules when present — obey the overlay.

## Ready gate

Copy is ready only when claim lint reports no forbidden hits. Soft claims ("faster for many readers") are OK when not presented as measured proof.
